Radial menu — visual demo

Click a card → the ring fans out (segments sweep out with a stagger), and it deliberately spills beyond the panel edge.

Click a segment to act:
• Edit → opens a card editor popup
• Complete → toggles (turns green)
• Due → opens editor (due)

Click outside or press Esc to close.

This is a browser preview of the look/interaction only — not wired to Trello.
